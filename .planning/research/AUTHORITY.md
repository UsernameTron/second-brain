# AUTHORITY — Source of Truth Hierarchy

> **Installed to:** `~/memory/_meta/AUTHORITY.md`
> **Read by:** every Claude surface at session start, via the CLAUDE.md router
> **Changed by:** Pete only, via the weekly pass, with a commit message stating why

This file answers one question: **when two sources disagree, which one wins?**

Without it, four things claim to know who Pete is — the auto-memory blob, userPreferences, the ABOUT ME canon, and CLAUDE.md — and none of them outrank the others. That is not memory. That is four rumors.

---

## The tiers

| Tier | Store | Authority | Writable by | Lives |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **T1** | `canon/` | **Highest.** True until explicitly changed. | Weekly pass only | `~/memory/canon/` |
| **T2** | `ledger/` | **Historical record.** Append-only. Never contradicts T1 — it *explains* it. | Weekly pass; append-only | `~/memory/ledger/` |
| **T3** | `working/` | **Provisional.** Live state, decays. Never promoted to fact without passing the T1 gate. | Weekly pass writes; hooks append to `_inbox/` | `~/memory/working/` |
| **T4** | Auto-memory blob | **Lowest. Non-authoritative cache.** | Nobody. Anthropic generates it. | Anthropic's system |

`procedural/` (the skill registry) is a **routing index**, not a fact store. It sits outside the tiers. It tells the model what capability exists and where — it never asserts truth about Pete.

---

## The rules

### A1 — Canon wins. Always.

On any conflict between `canon/` and any other source — including the auto-memory blob, including userPreferences, including something Claude said last week — **canon wins.**

No exceptions, no judgment call, no "but the blob is more recent." Recency is not authority. If canon is wrong, *fix canon*. Do not route around it.

### A2 — The auto-memory blob is a cache, and it is expected to drift.

The blob cannot be written to. Anthropic generates it in the background from conversation history, with a recency bias, and it flattens the difference between "Pete considered X" and "Pete decided X."

Therefore:

- **Never treat a blob assertion as authoritative.** It's a hint about what was recently discussed, nothing more.
- **When the blob contradicts canon, log it.** Append the contradiction to `_meta/memory-corrections.md`.
- **The weekly pass batches those corrections** into `memory_user_edits`. That's the only lever on the blob, and it is bounded (30 edits max). Spend the budget on contradictions that matter — identity, company, architecture, exclusions — not on trivia.

**Do not attempt to manage the blob as a store.** You will lose. Demote it and instrument it.

### A3 — One fact, one home. Structurally enforced.

Every fact-type resolves to exactly one file. Not "primarily" one file. Exactly one.

| Fact-type | Home | Example |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Who Pete is, how he works, his voice | `canon/identity.md` | Vibecoder posture; DFW; anti-AI-writing rules |
| Company, entity, role, engagement shape | `canon/company.md` | Connor Advisors LLC; embedded at CTG as VP Tech |
| Architecture decisions, standing technical rules | `canon/architecture.md` | Keyless ADC; `google-genai` with `vertexai=True` |
| Visual/design defaults | `canon/design-system.md` | Obsidian dark-mode tokens |
| Hard permanent exclusions | `canon/exclusions.md` | The three prohibited topics |
| What was decided, when, why, and its status | `ledger/decisions.md` | ADR-lite entries |
| What broke, root cause, and the rule that came out of it | `ledger/failures.md` | The enrichment MCP timeout scar tissue |
| Live project state, open loops, unresolved decisions | `working/open-loops.md` | Fred's data-custody call; W0-1 |
| What skills/agents exist and when to reach for them | `procedural/skill-registry.md` | Model-readable capability index |

**CLAUDE.md holds none of these.** CLAUDE.md is a router. It says *where to look*, never *what is true*. If a fact appears in CLAUDE.md, that is a defect — file it and move the fact home.

This is the fix for the `~/context/` duplication, and it is *structural*: you cannot have a conflict if only one file is allowed to hold the fact.

### A4 — Old locations get tombstones, not deletions.

When content moves out of `~/Claude Cowork/ABOUT ME/` or `~/context/`, leave a pointer stub:

```markdown
# MOVED

This content now lives at `~/memory/canon/identity.md`.
Do not edit this file. Do not read it as truth.
Moved 2026-07-19. See `git log` in ~/memory for history.
```

Deleting creates the "wait, where did that go" failure. Tombstoning creates a signpost. Cheap. Do it.

### A5 — Chat is capture-only.

claude.ai chat cannot read the filesystem. It therefore **cannot be authoritative and cannot be trusted to know canon.**

Anything decided in a chat session **does not exist** until it lands in `~/memory`. No exceptions. This is a limitation of the platform, not a design choice, and pretending otherwise is how knowledge dies.

Practical consequence: after a substantive chat session, export the decisions. The weekly pass has a step for this.

### A6 — Provenance survives. Status survives.

Every canon entry carries who said it and what state it's in. See `LIFECYCLE.md` for the frontmatter schema.

The specific failure this prevents: Claude proposes an approach, Pete says "interesting," and six weeks later the system reports it as a decision Pete made. **A suggestion is not a decision.** The `provenance:` field is what keeps that line intact.

## Conflict resolution — the actual procedure

When two sources disagree:

1. **Is one of them `canon/`?** → canon wins. Log the loser to `_meta/memory-corrections.md`. Done.
2. **Both in `canon/`?** → this is a defect in A3. One of them is in the wrong home. Fix the homing, don't pick a winner.
3. **Neither in canon (e.g., blob vs. working)?** → neither is authoritative. Ask Pete. Do not guess, and do not average.
4. **Canon disagrees with Pete, live, in-session?** → **Pete wins, and canon is stale.** Flag it immediately: *"canon/architecture.md says X, last verified 2026-04-02 — do you want me to update it?"* Never silently defer, and never silently override.

Rule 4 is the one that matters most. A memory system that asserts a stale fact with confidence is **worse than no memory system at all.** Confidently wrong beats forgetful for damage every single time.
